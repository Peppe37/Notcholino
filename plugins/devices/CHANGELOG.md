# Changelog

## [0.1.0-alpha] - 2026-10-15

### Added
- First release of Devices: the battery of the Bluetooth devices connected to your Mac (each AirPods earbud and the case, a mouse, a keyboard, a trackpad, a controller). The button shows the lowest one, colored by how low it is; the options list them all. It is read in the background, when the options open and every five minutes.
