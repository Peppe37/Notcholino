# Changelog

## [0.1.0-alpha] - 2026-10-14

### Added
- First release of BatLife: the Control Center button is the battery level, green, orange or red by how full it is, and blue while charging.
- Click it for the health (compared with a new battery), charge cycles, the power in use or charging, time left or until full, the temperature, and the charger's wattage.
- An optional graph of the power in use over the last six minutes.
- Reads the battery's own data, no admin rights needed. Macs without a battery show a message.
