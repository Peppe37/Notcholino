# Changelog

## [0.1.0-alpha] - 2026-10-15

### Added
- First release of Reader: click the button, drag over any text on the screen, and it is copied to the clipboard, with a popup saying how many words. It uses the system's screenshot tool (macOS asks for Screen Recording the first time) and the built-in text recognition: the picture is deleted at once and nothing leaves your Mac. Needs Notcholino 0.11.0 or later.
