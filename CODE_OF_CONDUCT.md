# Code of Conduct

Notcholino follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity, age, or abilities.

## What we expect

- Be respectful, patient and constructive, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and in a place where others can find them (issues and Discussions).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention, and any unwelcome contact.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who do. To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Peppe37/Notcholino/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through the profile at [github.com/Peppe37](https://github.com/Peppe37). Reports are handled privately.
