# Snap

## [0.2.0-alpha] - 2026-10-20

- **The notch opens when you shake the window**, not every time you drag one: grab a window and move it as you like, and shake it from side to side when you want to snap it. The old behaviour (open on every drag) is a choice in Settings → Extensions → Snap → The notch opens.

## [0.1.1-alpha] - 2026-10-19

- Notcholino's own windows (Settings) can be snapped like any other window. Only real windows are taken: the notch's own panels and other floating things are not.

## [0.1.0-alpha] - 2026-10-19

- First version: grab a window and the notch opens with a small screen to drop it on. Halves, quarters and the whole screen, with a preview, on any display.
